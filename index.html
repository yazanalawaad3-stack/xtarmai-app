Rules (click)
  • Local-only simulation using demo points.
  • Fixed sequence: Loss, Loss, Win (repeat).
  • On loss: balance decreases by stake; pool increases by 70% of stake; fee is 30% of stake.
  • On win: payout = 30% of current pool; balance increases by payout; pool resets to 0.
  • All data stored in your browser (localStorage). No server calls except price fetch.
