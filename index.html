Scenario Trading Demo (Personal)

Personal demo for UI testing and fun. No real trading. No real funds.
Outcomes are scenario-based and manually selected before opening a position. If a trade ends with positive scenario PnL, it is split 70% to House and 30% to Player. Live price is shown from Binance public stream (WebSocket) with REST fallback.
